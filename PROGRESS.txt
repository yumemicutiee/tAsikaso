WHERE THE APP IS TODAY
----------------------
tAsikaso is a study app for Android (and the web) with:
- Pomodoro timer with Focus Mode, breaks, tasks, statistics and analysis
- "When Time Is Up": Alarm Clock (the phone's alarm sound), Vibration
  Only, Alarm Clock and Vibration, or Off; rings even when the app is closed
- Scanning notes with auto-crop that finds the page on any phone (AI
  models inside the app, no internet needed), text recognition, and PDFs
  whose text can be selected and searched
- Smart Study with Claude (Opus 5.5): typed-up notes, summaries, key
  concepts, flashcards, quizzes, and typed answers judged by meaning
- Flashcards with smart review, quizzes, library and folders, search
- Google Drive sync, backup and restore, dark mode, app colours
- tAsikaso+ plans (test purchases for now; real payments not connected)

Progress: 5 of 8 milestones done (A, B, B2, R and most of C); the app can
be installed and used today.


MILESTONES
----------
[DONE] A. Robustness (25 Oct, v0.4.2)
       Auto-crop safety checks, large Drive uploads, double-tap guards,
       small screens and large text, more emulator tests.

[DONE] B. Smart Study live (18 Nov)
       Study server deployed on Render (Singapore), connected to Claude
       Opus 5.5 with the owner's key, rebuilds itself on every push.
       First real costs: about PHP 6.50 per one-page study kit, about
       PHP 0.18 per answer check. Built into the app since v0.5.0.
       Left for the owner: set a monthly spend limit in the Claude Console.

[DONE] B2. Auto-crop for every phone (18 Nov)
       DocAligner page-corner models run inside the app (big model, then
       with a border, then the small model, plus a guard against boxes
       printed on the page). On 96 test photos: 90 right, 5 wrong (the old
       detector found 60). About 0.5 s per photo on the Android emulator.
       Left: test on 10-20 real photos of real notes.

[DONE] R. Release v0.5.0 (18 Nov) and v0.5.1 (18 Nov)
       Signed with the upload key; install over older versions keeps data.

[MOSTLY DONE] C. Verified without a phone (changed 18 Nov)
       A real-phone run-through wasn't possible, so every item of the phone
       checklist is checked by automated tests, an Android 14 emulator on
       GitHub after every push, and checks on the built APK.
       Passing on the emulator:
         - saving on the phone and after a restart; backup and restore
         - text recognition and selectable text in the Scan PDF
         - timer alerts, a real 1-minute Pomodoro in Focus Mode
         - alerts when exact alarms are NOT allowed (Android 14 default):
           still arrive (about 1 s late on the emulator)
         - notifications refused: alerts switch off and the app says why
         - camera to PDF, gallery import, auto-crop on hard photos
         - Smart Study end to end (app -> server -> Claude stand-in):
           cards, quiz, answer check, wrong token, no server; about 6 s
         - a 12-page scan to one searchable PDF (about 1-1.5 min, memory flat)
         - the APK: only approved permissions, ready for Android 15+ phones
       Unit and widget tests: 237 (app) and 63 (server), all passing.
       Still being checked: the alarm channels of "When Time Is Up" on the
       emulator (sound, alarm volume, vibration), and timing on a slow,
       cheap-phone emulator profile.
       Only a real phone can confirm (known risks): Google's own document
       scanner screen, real camera photos and handwriting, hearing the
       alarm and feeling the vibration, battery savers on some brands
       delaying alerts, sharing a backup to the Drive app.

[IN PROGRESS] D. Ready for other students
       Done (18 Nov):
         - privacy policy, linked in Settings and Help
         - crash reports to the study server's log (never notes or names),
           with a switch in Settings
         - daily Smart Study limits per phone instead of per Wi-Fi network
         - unused fingerprint permissions removed
       Left for the owner (a few clicks each):
         - Google Cloud Console: take Google sign-in out of "Testing"
           (today only listed testers can sign in, and they are signed out
           after 7 days); the privacy policy URL above is ready for it
         - GitHub: add the signing secrets so the automatic test builds
           can install over releases
       Known limits: one shared app token, limits reset when the server
       restarts, a $5/day spending cap for the whole server (real accounts
       come with milestone E).

[NOT STARTED] E. Paid plans for real
       Real payments instead of test purchases, and accounts on the server
       so Free vs Plus is enforced there too. Needs the owner's decisions.

[NOT STARTED] F. Launch 1.0
       Distribution, version 1.0, announcement.
