DocAligner page-corner models
=============================

tAsikaso's auto-crop uses two of DocAligner's heatmap models, by Ze Yuan /
DocsaidLab: https://github.com/DocsaidLab/DocAligner

Both models:
Input:  "img", float32 [1, 3, 256, 256], RGB, values 0-1, the whole photo
        resized (stretched) to 256 x 256.
Output: "heatmap", float32 [1, 4, 128, 128], one map per corner in the order
        top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left.
