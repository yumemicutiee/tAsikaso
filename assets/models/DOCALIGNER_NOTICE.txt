docaligner_lcnet100.onnx
========================

The page-corner model used by tAsikaso's auto-crop is DocAligner's
"lcnet100" heatmap model (file lcnet100_h_e_bifpn_256_fp32.onnx, renamed),
by Ze Yuan / DocsaidLab: https://github.com/DocsaidLab/DocAligner

SHA-256: F4117B786E3A18470F3865C93F3C2BD69D9B998EDD60F385574A5C665E79594E

Code license: Apache License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

Model weights: the author stated in
https://github.com/DocsaidLab/DocAligner/issues/11 (1 October 2026):
"For the official DocAligner model weights/checkpoints, feel free to use
them however you like. I do not impose any restrictions on their use,
including commercial use, redistribution, conversion, quantization,
fine-tuning, or redistribution of derived checkpoints." The author also
noted that the third-party datasets used to train it are not his to
license.

Citation requested by the author:
  @misc{yuan2023docaligner, author = {Ze Yuan}, title = {DocAligner},
        year = {2023}, publisher = {GitHub},
        url = {https://github.com/DocsaidLab/DocAligner}}

Input:  "img", float32 [1, 3, 256, 256], RGB, values 0-1, the whole photo
        resized (stretched) to 256 x 256.
Output: "heatmap", float32 [1, 4, 128, 128], one map per corner in the order
        top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left.
