tAsikaso Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026 · applies to the tAsikaso app for Android and the web version at yumemicutiee.github.io/tAsikaso

In short: your notes, scanned pages and flashcards are kept on your phone. Only when you use Smart Study are the photos of the pages you scan (so Claude can read the text in them) and the text from your notes sent to our study server and to Anthropic (the company that makes Claude) to write your study materials. Neither keeps them for us. With Read Photos with Claude off, only text is sent; with Smart Study off, nothing is. Your tAsikaso account, kept with Supabase, holds your name and photo, settings, tasks, Pomodoro statistics, plan and daily Smart Study use, so they come back when you sign in again; never your notes. If you sign in with Google, a copy of your data is kept in a private folder of your own Google Drive. We don't sell data, show ads, or track you.

What stays on your phone

Folders, notes (PDFs), photos you scan, flashcards and quizzes are saved only in the app's own storage on your phone (or, on the web, in your browser). Your name, settings, tasks and Pomodoro statistics are saved there too, and copied to your account (below). Uninstalling the app or Settings → Delete All Data removes them from the phone.

These features work entirely on your phone, with no internet:

  • Finding the page edges (auto-crop) uses AI models built into the app (DocAligner). Your photos are not sent anywhere.
  • Finding the lines of text in your photos (the blue boxes while a page is read) uses Google's ML Kit text recognition, which runs on the phone. Without Smart Study, or with Read Photos with Claude off, ML Kit also reads the text itself.
  • Timer alerts and reminders are scheduled on the phone.

On Android, the Smart Scanner button opens Google's document scanner, provided by Google Play services on your phone. Google's own terms apply to that screen.

Smart Study (Claude)

When Smart Study is on and you scan pages, save a scan, ask for AI flashcards or a quiz, type up a note, or check a typed quiz answer, the app sends:

  • with Read Photos with Claude on (the default), the photo of each page you scan, cropped to the page, so Claude can write out the text on it (it reads handwriting much better than the phone can),
  • the text of your pages, the note's title, your flashcards, and your typed quiz answers,
  • to our study server (hosted by Render, in Singapore), which passes them to Anthropic's Claude API and sends back the text on a page, typed-up notes, a summary, key concepts, flashcards, quiz questions or a verdict on your answer.

Anthropic handles these photos and this text under its commercial terms and privacy policy: it doesn't use it to train its models, and it keeps API inputs and outputs only for a limited time (normally up to 30 days) for safety and abuse monitoring. See anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Our study server keeps none of your photos or text. For each request it writes one log line: the time, which feature was used, whether it worked, how long it took, how many tokens Claude used and what that cost, and a short one-way code made from your internet address (not the address itself). Your internet address is kept only in the server's memory, to apply the daily limits, and is gone when the server restarts. Our host keeps these log lines for a limited time.

You can turn Read Photos with Claude off in Settings → Smart Study; then your photos aren't sent, only text read on the phone. You can turn Smart Study off there too; then nothing is sent and the app reads text, makes flashcards and quizzes on the phone instead.

Your tAsikaso account

So that your settings and statistics come back when you sign in again, and Smart Study's daily limits and your tAsikaso+ plan follow you (and can't be used up by someone else), the app makes a tAsikaso account: without a name or email at first, or linked to your Google account once you Continue with Google. It is kept by Supabase (our account provider) in Singapore and holds only:

  • an account id, and for a Google account your Google name and email address,
  • your profile: the name and photo you use in the app,
  • your settings (colours, theme, timer and alarm choices, reminder, Smart Study switches) and Pomodoro lengths,
  • your Pomodoro tasks and statistics: each finished Pomodoro and break (when and how long, and the task's name), and how many minutes you focused and cards you reviewed each day,
  • your plan (which one, from when, until when), and later your payments,
  • how many Smart Study requests, answer checks and pages read by Claude you used each day, and their estimated cost.

It is saved a few seconds after a change and before you log out. When you Log Out of a Google account, these are cleared from the phone and come back when you continue with Google again. Your notes, scanned pages and flashcards are not stored there. Only you can read or change your own account data. Settings → Your Data → Delete Account deletes the account, its data, plan and usage at once (records of payments, once real payments exist, are kept without your name, for the books). Supabase's privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy.

Google sign-in and Drive sync

Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, the app receives your Google name, email address and profile photo (to show on your Profile) and permission for one private folder in your Google Drive that only tAsikaso can see (drive.appdata). It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

Backup & Sync puts a copy of your tAsikaso data (folders, notes, photos, flashcards, tasks, statistics, settings) in that folder so you can restore it on another phone. It goes straight from your phone to Google; it doesn't pass through our server. Google's privacy policy applies to your Google account and Drive.

To remove that copy: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps, find tAsikaso and choose Delete hidden app data. Signing out of the app or Delete All Data leaves the Drive copy alone, on purpose, so you don't lose it by accident.

Backup files

Back Up Now makes a single file with your data and opens your phone's share sheet. Where it goes (Google Drive, email, a chat) is your choice. The file never includes your study server token.

Crash reports

If the app runs into an error, it can send a short report to our study server so we can fix it: the error message and where in the app's code it happened, the app version and the Android or browser version. It never includes your notes, photos, flashcards, name or email. You can turn this off in Settings → Your Data → Send Crash Reports.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing data with advertisers.
  • No profiles for advertising: the only account data we keep is described under Your tAsikaso account.
  • Payments for tAsikaso+ aren't connected yet; when they are, this page will say how they're handled.

Children

tAsikaso is made for students. If you are under 13, please use it with a parent's or guardian's permission, especially Smart Study and Google sign-in.
